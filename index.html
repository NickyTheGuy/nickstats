How classifications work

For every row/column pair, the tool compares the row player’s matches with the column player against all of the row player’s other matches. The impact score is 80% win-rate delta and 20% rating delta. ADR and K/D do not affect classifications. Small comparison groups are automatically shrunk toward Exister.

Lifter means an adjusted score of at least +0.25; Dragger means −0.25 or lower; everything between is Exister. These are descriptive associations, not proof that one teammate caused the change. Rank differences, maps, opponents, and overlapping parties can all matter.
